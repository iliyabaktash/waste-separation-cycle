A home recycling record

How our waste finds its way back into the cycle

Every household throws things away. What happens in the ten seconds before that is the part worth watching — the sorting, the separating, the small decisions that keep material out of a landfill.

The full separation cycle, start to finish.

The cycle, step by step

01

Collect

Waste is gathered from the kitchen, bathroom, and any other room before it's sorted.

02

Separate

Items are split into categories — organic, plastic, paper, glass, and metal — each into its own container.

03

Rinse and prep

Containers and packaging are rinsed and flattened where needed, so nothing contaminates the batch it's placed in.

04

Store

Sorted waste is held in its designated bin until collection day.

05

Hand off

Each category goes out to the correct collection point or pickup, ready to be processed and reused.
