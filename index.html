Project Record — DWG‑004

Waste Separation Conveyor System

TTS Group · Sari, Iran · 2023–2024

ME

A 12‑line conveyor system designed for a waste‑separation plant in Sari — from 3D modeling and structural analysis through to fabrication drawings and on‑site installation.

Scope of work

  • Simulated and 3D‑modeled the 12‑line conveyor system in Sidewinder and SolidWorks
  • Ran finite element analysis on the conveyor structure in SolidWorks Simulation
  • Calculated power requirement, drum/idler diameter and belt tension in MATLAB
  • Produced the bill of materials, cutting plans and construction drawings for the system
